# Vision

## Long-Term Vision

English Reading Coach will become a daily practice companion for learners who want to speak English with better rhythm, confidence, and clarity. The core experience is guided reading: users see a sentence, follow word-by-word highlighting, control the pace, complete focused lessons, and build a habit through visible progress.

Future AI features may listen, evaluate pronunciation, and coach users. Those features must enhance the reading experience, not replace it. The product should always feel focused, respectful, and practical.

## Phase 1 Experience Promise

The first release should feel like opening a quiet, well-designed practice room. The user should immediately know what to read, how fast the reading is moving, how much time remains, and what progress they have made.

## Product Pillars

### Focus

The reading screen is the center of the product. It should minimize distractions and keep the user in a smooth rhythm.

### Clarity

Users should understand lesson difficulty, category, duration, completion status, and streak progress without needing explanations.

### Control

Users can adjust speed, pause, resume, restart, and navigate through lessons without losing progress.

### Encouragement

Progress indicators should motivate consistent practice without shaming missed days.

### Extensibility

The architecture must allow speech recognition, AI feedback, generated lessons, and coaching workflows later without rewriting the core reading system.

## Future Product Direction

Phase 2 can introduce voice capture and pronunciation feedback. Phase 3 can introduce AI-assisted coaching, personalized lesson generation, and adaptive difficulty. Version 2.0 can support richer learner profiles, teacher workflows, and multilingual onboarding.

## Experience Principles

- The user always knows what is happening.
- Reading controls are reachable on mobile.
- Typography is comfortable for prolonged reading.
- Highlighting is synchronized, legible, and accessible.
- Statistics explain progress in human terms.
- Empty states help users take the next action.
- Errors are calm, specific, and recoverable.
